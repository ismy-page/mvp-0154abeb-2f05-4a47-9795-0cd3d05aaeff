Process

From still image to speaking subject.

A considerate workflow that respects source material while delivering convincing, characterful motion.

Curate & clear

Identify artwork, verify rights/usage, and gather references.

Rig & stylize

Prepare a facial rig aligned to brushwork and texture.

Voice & sync

Source or record voiceover; generate expressive lip‑sync.

Grade & deliver

Color, grain, and framing to match the original piece; export to spec.
